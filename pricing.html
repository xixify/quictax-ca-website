Can I customize my package with add-ons?

▼

Yes! The basic packages listed above can be customized with specialized add-ons (such as rental property schedules, crypto gain reporting, prior year catch-ups, or HST returns) to suit your unique situation.

▼

Fees for standalone tax returns are billed on a flat, transparent upfront basis before NETFILE submission. Fees for monthly accounting tiers (Silver, Gold, Platinum) are billed conveniently on a monthly subscription basis with zero long-term lock-in contracts.

▼

We specialize in helping Canadians get up to date with unfiled taxes! We prepare prior year T1 or T2 returns at $199 per year and evaluate whether you qualify for the Voluntary Disclosures Program (VDP) to eliminate CRA late-filing penalties.

No! Unlike traditional accountants who bill by the hour or charge $15 per extra T4 slip, QuicTax provides 100% transparent quotes. What we quote on WhatsApp is what you pay.
